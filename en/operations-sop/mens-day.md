International Men's Day is celebrated annually on November 19 (falling on Thursday, November 19, 2026) to highlight men's and boys' health, celebrate positive role models, and address social challenges. In 2026, the International Council for Men and Boys launched its inaugural Global State of Men and Boys Report 2026 alongside advocacy around the theme "The Future Is Everyone."
